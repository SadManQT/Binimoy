## Limitations

- **Objective release is off in all three templates.** Every capability is `UNCONFIGURED` until its adapter is built and tested, so no template can prove its mandatory items independently yet.
- No funding, so the "only while unfunded" guard on withdrawal does not exist yet (LP-005). Withdrawal is currently allowed from `ACCEPTED`.
- A deal can select "human from the start", but no reviewer can be assigned (LP-010). The page says so.
- Amendments after acceptance are not implemented; an accepted agreement can only be withdrawn.
- Agreement expiry (`ExpireAgreement`) has no timer yet.
- Payout destination is not part of the terms yet; it arrives with the ledger (LP-005) and must be added to the snapshot then.
- The invitation token appears in the inviter's URL after creation, so it is in that browser's history.
- Amount entry accepts ASCII digits only; Bangla numerals are refused rather than guessed.
- An invited person who is not signed in is sent to `/login` and must reopen the link afterwards.
- `createDeal` has no idempotency key; a double submit creates two drafts.
- Pages are functional and unstyled (LP-014).
