# Handoff: LP-010 Disputes, human review, appeals

- Base: `main` after LP-008/LP-009 first slice
- Branch: `chunk-review`
- Date: 2026-10-12

## Implemented

**Three ways a dispute ends** (`packages/db/src/review.ts`)

1. **Mutual resolution** (`proposeResolution`, `acceptResolutionProposal`): one party proposes what the seller gets, the rest goes to the buyer. The other party accepts that exact proposal. A replaced proposal cannot be accepted. Path `MUTUAL_RESOLUTION`.
2. **Reviewed resolution**: `requestReview` queues the case; `claimReview` gives it to one staff reviewer; `decide` records the rule, the facts, the rejected inferences, the reasoning and an allocation of exactly the held amount. The decision waits for the appeal window. `appealDecision` holds it and sends it to a different reviewer, whose decision is final. `waiveAppeal` lets both parties accept early. `finalizeDecisions` (worker) applies unchallenged decisions. Path `REVIEWED_RESOLUTION`.
3. **Unresolved**: a reviewer may find the facts cannot be established, or an AI-only dispute runs out its seven-day budget (`closeUnresolvedAiOnly`). The money stays held and nobody is declared the winner. `reopenDispute` reopens it.

**Reviewer controls (D.2, D.6)**
- A reviewer must hold an active `reviewer` staff role and must not be a party to the deal.
- One active reviewer per case, enforced by a unique index; a race has one winner.
- A case is readable only by its currently assigned reviewer (`getCase`). Releasing the assignment or revoking the staff role ends the access, including mid-case.
- The original reviewer cannot claim or decide the appeal.
- A reviewer never moves money. Every outcome becomes an authorization for the settlement engine, limited to what is held.

**Service modes (B2)**
- AI-only: review cannot be requested.
- Human-from-start: `claimDeal` lets a reviewer join an accepted deal; funding is refused until one has. A dispute on such a deal goes straight to that reviewer.

**Web, worker, operator tool**
- Deal page: resolution section (dispute state, decisions with their reasons, appeal, accept decision, proposals, request review, reopen).
- `/review` (queue) and `/review/[id]` (case and decision form). Both return 404 to anyone who is not an eligible, assigned reviewer.
- Worker: finalize decisions, close AI-only disputes.
- `pnpm staff grant|revoke|list`: staff roles are granted from the command line and recorded with the operator's name. There is no in-app way to become staff.

## Not built

- **Exceptional adjustments and the second approver** (B10, D.6). No exceptional action exists at all, so nothing can bypass the held-amount limit; the approver role is unused.
- **Objective release** (the fourth B9 path).
- **Reviewer workload and competence matching.** Any eligible reviewer can take any queued case.
- **Assignment expiry and handoff.** An assignment lasts until released by an outcome or a revoked role.
- **MFA for staff** (D.2).
- **Evidence attachments and the case builder** (FE-21). A dispute carries a claim type and free text only.
- **Post-settlement claims.** A dispute opened after dispatch is recorded, but a decision on it cannot allocate money that has already left.
- **Notices.** Parties learn of a decision by opening the page; nothing is sent.

## Not run

- Independent review.
- The browser tests in CI.
- A real reviewer using the case page with a keyboard or screen reader.

## Limitations

- The decision form uses single-line inputs for long text.
- A decision's appeal deadline is fixed when the decision is recorded; nothing extends it if a required notice fails, because notices do not exist yet (D.6).
- `finalizeDecisions` skips a decision whose allocation no longer equals the held amount and leaves the dispute awaiting; nothing alerts anyone.
